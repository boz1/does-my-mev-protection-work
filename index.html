Private Sandwich Lookup

Paste an Ethereum transaction hash. If a persistent bot sandwiched it after it was sent privately, you will see the attack, the order-flow auctions (OFAs) the transaction showed up in, and the bot behind it. The dataset is the one from No Place to Hide: An Analysis on Protected Order Flow Sandwich Attacks (arXiv:2609.28115).

Try: MEV-Share + MEVBlocker · Blink only · MEVBlocker only · unattributed · a bot's front leg

Scope. 30,607 sandwiches by 7 persistent bots against 39,461 private Ethereum swaps, 1 July 2023 to 30 June 2026 (Table 3 of the paper). Uniswap V2–V4 and identical forks only. A transaction counts as private if it never reached the public mempool according to the Flashbots mempool archive. Public-mempool sandwiches, reorg-exposed sandwiches, bots below the persistence thresholds, and other chains are not in this dataset. If your hash is missing here, that does not mean you were not sandwiched.

OFA labels are positive evidence only: a label means the transaction appears in that provider's records. Coverage: MEV-Share 2022-02 to 2026-07 (Dune, data browser and event stream combined), MEVBlocker 2023-04 to 2026-07, Blink from BuilderNet bundle data 2025-08 to 2026-07, Merkle 2025-01 to 2026-03. A missing label does not rule a route out. Profits are gross token round trips before gas and fees. The paper reports USD figures per bot, not per sandwich.
